# Privacy Policy

## Introduction
This privacy policy explains how the Horse Breeding Tracker application collects, uses, stores, and protects information provided by users.

## Information We Collect
When you create an account or sign in, we may collect:
- your username
- your password (stored as a securely hashed value)
- breeding records, stallion records, notes, shots, and other data you enter into the application

## How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- provide account access and authentication
- save and display your breeding records
- allow you to manage your data within the application

## Data Storage
User information and records are stored in the application’s server-side database and local browser storage as needed. Access to your data is limited to the account currently signed in.

## Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only as required by law, to operate the service, or to provide hosting and technical support.

## Your Choices
You may sign out of the application at any time. If you want your account data removed, contact the service operator.

## Security
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep your login credentials private.

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact the service operator.
